Battle Report (Hero Damage / Damage Taken)

Have the game's post-match "Data" screen up on your monitor, then hit Capture — this reads straight off the screen through the same fast per-region OCR your live kills/gold HUD uses, no image upload needed. First time only: calibrate the 20 regions once with the command below (like you did for the HUD), it's reused after that.

cd ocr && python calibrate.py postgame_dealt_team1_0 postgame_taken_team1_0 postgame_dealt_team1_1 postgame_taken_team1_1 postgame_dealt_team1_2 postgame_taken_team1_2 postgame_dealt_team1_3 postgame_taken_team1_3 postgame_dealt_team1_4 postgame_taken_team1_4 postgame_dealt_team2_0 postgame_taken_team2_0 postgame_dealt_team2_1 postgame_taken_team2_1 postgame_dealt_team2_2 postgame_taken_team2_2 postgame_dealt_team2_3 postgame_taken_team2_3 postgame_dealt_team2_4 postgame_taken_team2_4

Graphic Fixing

Drag a logo or name to reposition it; drag the little gold handle on its corner to resize. This preview updates instantly, but nothing changes on the real overlay until you click Save Positions — safe to experiment. Positions are per-element nudges on top of the normal layout, not a replacement for it.

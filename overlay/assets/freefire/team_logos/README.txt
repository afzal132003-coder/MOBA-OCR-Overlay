TEAM LOGOS -- one image per team, named after the team.

  CLUTZA ESPORTS.png   or   CTZ.png   (full name or short name;
  case, spaces and punctuation are ignored)

PNG (transparent is best), JPG or WEBP. The engine reads this folder every
10 seconds: a new file shows on the next check, and replacing a file
updates that team's logo. A file here wins over a logo uploaded in the
dashboard's Team Info.

The images stay on this PC (they are not pushed to GitHub): the engine
sends each logo to the graphics itself.
